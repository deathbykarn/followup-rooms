01

The cohort ladder.

Pricing

Pricing is a time ladder, not a feature grid. Everyone gets the same product. What changes is the price you lock in by joining early. Reward the believers; let the standard rate rise behind them.

Phase 1 · months 0–3
Beta
$0free
  • Pilot cohort, ~5–10 agents (KW wedge)
  • Free access in exchange for feedback + a case-study reference
  • Time-boxed (~3 months)
  • No card required to start
  • Converts to the Founder price when beta ends
Unlocked from beta · locked 12 months
Founder
$99/ mo
  • Beta members + early signups (cohort capped, ~first 50 paying)
  • Price locked for 12 months — a third below standard
  • Full product, no feature gating
  • Loyalty moat: they joined cheapest, they stay longest
  • Rolls to Pro rate after the 12-month lock
Standard · everyone after
Pro
$149/ mo
  • The going rate once the Founder cohort fills
  • Anchored against the ~$3,000/mo PA cost (≈5% of it)
  • Sits mid-band of the validated $100–$200 range
  • Team / Agency multi-seat tiers layer on later
Why this shape. Free beta removes every reason not to try. The Founder lock converts gratitude into a 12-month commitment at a price that still clears 90% margin. Pro lets the standard rate climb without ever re-pricing the believers. The discount is real and time-bound — honest scarcity, not theatrics.

07

Assumptions & sensitivities.

Read before trusting

Every number above rests on these. Change one and tell me — the model moves predictably.

  • Founder draw = $10,000/mo. The single biggest swing factor. At $5K draw, breakeven roughly halves (~42 seats blended) and $20K profit lands ~3 months earlier. At $0 draw, you're profitable from the first paying customer.
  • Ops = $10/seat, modelled conservative. Real cost likely $4–$7. If it holds at $6, contribution per Pro seat rises to $143 and every seat-count target drops ~3%.
  • Pricing is a hypothesis, not validated. Suresh gave a $100–$200 band; Scott named no price. $99 Founder / $149 Pro are reasoned placements. The beta exists partly to discover the real number — expect to move it.
  • Seat trajectory assumes the wave works. 250 seats by M24 = ~0.8% of SG's registered agents, riding the KW-then-PropNex cluster dynamics. If adoption is half as fast, $20K profit slips to ~M30 — still a sound single-founder business.
  • Blended ARPU drifts up over time. Early book is Founder-heavy ($99–$110); as 12-month locks expire and new Pro joins, the blend climbs toward $135–$149. The journey table bakes this in.
  • Production infra holds to ~$78/mo well past 250 seats. Render/Vercel/Supabase scale gently; the next step-up (dedicated DB, queue infra) only matters around 500+ active seats and is folded into the M36 line.
  • Beta is genuinely free and time-boxed (~3 months). If beta runs longer, the only added cost is infra+ops (cheap) — but the revenue clock starts later, pushing every milestone right by the overrun.
  • One-time setup ~$340 (Pte Ltd + domain) is excluded from the monthly tables. Meta Business Verification is free but gates the pilot start — submit early.
