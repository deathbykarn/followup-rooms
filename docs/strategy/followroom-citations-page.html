The promise underneath

FollowRoom remembers like a good assistant, not a rumour mill.

Every detail it hands you comes with the words behind it, the original is never altered, and you approve anything a client ever sees. The receipts are for you — so you never repeat something you're not sure of. The rest of this page shows how, and what it feels like on a Tuesday between viewings.

06

Why the receipt matters here — not later.

The payoff

The quote isn't there for an audit you'll never run. It's there so that when you're about to act on a detail — quote a number, walk into a viewing, send a follow-up — you can trust it without second-guessing.

Before a viewing

Walk in already on the same page.

Glance at the room and see what they care about — and the words they said it in. No cold opens.

On a call

Settle “1.6 or 1.7?” instantly.

You're reading their own words, not your memory of them. The number is right because you can see where it came from.

Writing a follow-up

Nothing you promised slips.

Every promise is logged with the message it came from — so “send the ABSD breakdown by Friday” doesn't quietly disappear.

The line that ties it together

You're never repeating a detail and hoping it's right. You're repeating it because you can see exactly where it came from.

PLACEMENT — best as a trust / “how it works” explainer inside the product or onboarding, or a section after the main landing page's safety block. Voice and lexicon follow followroom-positioning.html — adviser-calm, outcome-first, “receipt” as the everyday word for what we internally call citations. No mention of “AI”, “digital”, or any sibling product.

HONESTY GUARDRAIL — copy deliberately says “the words they said it in” / “the exact quote”, NOT “verified word-for-word”. Today the system attaches a quote and instructs the model to copy it verbatim, but it does not yet programmatically check that the quote is a literal substring of the original. Before this page can claim “every quote is verified against the original, character for character”, ship the small _insert_fact substring-check hardening — then the stronger claim becomes provably true. Until then, the page is true as written.
