01

The opportunity.

Context first

Relationship-driven salespeople win or lose on what they remember between meetings — and nothing they have captures it. FollowRoom is the memory layer that does, built to fit how they already work.

A property agent juggles 50–200 live relationships across WhatsApp, voice notes, and viewings. Deals close when the client felt remembered; they die in the gaps when the texture slips. CRMs demand data entry and miss the texture. A $3,000/month assistant forgets too.

FollowRoom turns forwarded messages and recorded meetings into a living, client-specific memory — every detail linked to its source — and a polished, co-branded room the agent shares with the client. Silent by design: it never messages the client; nothing reaches them without the operator's approval.

The wedge is Singapore real estate (31,500 registered agents); the ceiling is the ~500K relationship-sales operators across SG, Australia, Hong Kong and the Gulf who share the same shape.

The product is already built. Two qualified industry figures independently validated it — one designed the product, one designed the go-to-market. We are days, not quarters, from a paying pilot. This document is the offering; the full business plan is its foundation.

Why now, why this founder.

Timing & fit
i.

The tech crossed the line

WhatsApp's Cloud API matured; diarised transcription fell below $1/hour; LLM extraction with caching dropped to a few dollars per seat. The category was impossible to build affordably two years ago.

ii.

The buyer is ready

Singapore real estate's “agents as entrepreneurs” turn — led by Keller Williams' 2025 launch — primed the market for relationship-first, branding-led tooling over the old numbers game.

iii.

Founder-market fit

A former real estate operator with an active SG agency network — the warm relationships the pilot is built on — who can also build the product. The validation calls came from his own network.
