The take — in three sentences.

Synopsis

We replace the $3K / mo PA for the salespeople who live or die by what they remember between meetings.

Forwarded WhatsApp → diarised transcripts → AI-extracted facts → operator-approved client-facing room. Built on WhatsApp Cloud API, AssemblyAI, and Anthropic. Silent ingestion. Evidence receipts. No outbound. The operator owns the relationship. We own the memory.

04

The product — in four steps.

Already built

The operator forwards. FollowRoom remembers. The client opens a polished, branded room they keep coming back to.

STEP 01

Silent ingestion.

Operator forwards a WhatsApp message or uploads a meeting recording. No friction, no channel change.

›
STEP 02

Memory built.

AssemblyAI diarises. Claude extracts facts, each linked to the source message or transcript timestamp.

›
STEP 03

Operator approves.

Dashboard surfaces extracted facts. Operator accepts, rejects, edits. Tier-3 inferences never reach client surfaces.

›
STEP 04

Client opens.

Co-branded shareable link. Threaded follow-up timeline. Every claim traces to evidence. The client keeps coming back.

Risks — named, with mitigations.

What could break this

We do not pretend any of these aren’t real. Each is designed-against; none, in our judgement, is existential. The most dangerous risks in early-stage companies are the unnamed ones.

R-01TAM concentration
SG real estate alone is ~31,500 operators. Phase 3 vertical/geo expansion adds upside but complexity. Mitigated: unit economics work at small scale; profitability does not depend on TAM-scale outcomes.
R-02Meta dependency
Silent ingestion sits on WhatsApp Cloud API rails. Policy or rate-limit shifts could disrupt onboarding. Mitigated: ingestion is channel-agnostic at the boundary; Telegram + email forwarding parity is a Phase 2.5 lift.
R-03Median-user activation
Our design partner Scott is a ceiling user. Median agents need more hand-holding. Mitigated: pilot cohort hand-selected; live setup support; activation funnel instrumented from day one.
R-04AI cost volatility
Anthropic + AssemblyAI pricing not fixed. A 2× increase compresses margin to ~88%. Mitigated: prompt caching reduces extraction ~70%; transcription is a swappable commodity.
R-05Single-founder bandwidth
Khaniff is solo to Month 6. Mitigated: first revenue funds first hire. Phase 1 designed to be operationally light. Doctrine + tooling reduces solo execution risk vs. typical pre-seed.
R-06GTM cycle length
Agency-level deals are 6–18 month cycles. Mitigated: per-seat economics ($149) close at the individual level. Bottom-up adoption, top-down legitimisation. We never depend on agency-level deals.
R-07Compliance creep
Phase 3 verticals (insurance, private banking) require PDPA, GDPR, MAS overlays. Mitigated: never enter a vertical without a paid design partner; compliance cost is in Phase 3 budget, not Phase 2.
R-08Adjacent incumbents
Salesforce (KW partner), HubSpot, agency-internal builds. Mitigated: stay sharper, smaller, channel-native, trust-first. Compete on what they won’t accept: silent ingestion, Tier-3 filter, evidence receipts.
